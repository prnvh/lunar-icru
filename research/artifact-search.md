# Artifact search, 2026-09-29

The comparison gate needs a second model that can be re-run, not another narrative restatement. This search looked for workbooks, supplements, and tabulated cash flows. It did not use shadow-library copies.

| Target | What was checked | Result |
|---|---|---|

| Sowers 2021, New Space, DOI 10.1089/space.2020.0045, including the SMAD PDF copy | Table 9 and the cumulative-cash prose | Same IRRs, 8.84%, 15.8%, and 15.4%. No annual series. Not a second model. |
| Mines release and grant 80NSSC19K0964 | Release page and public web | PDF only. No supplemental workbook found. |

| Jones et al., IAC 2019, lunar-surface architectures, NTRS 20200002704 and the Big Idea PDF | Text of the public paper, including sections 3–5 and Table 3 | Distinct from `jones2020`. Lander and plant costs are PCEC response surfaces. The surfaces are not printed as coefficients. Equation 1 did not survive text extraction as a usable formula. Contour figures are the campaign results. They were not digitized. This paper does not supply the 2020 campaign ledger and does not pass its own reconstruction gate. |
| Jones, Klovstad, Judd, and Komar, AIAA 2019-1372, cis-lunar propellant | Public PDF at the Big Idea and RASCAL mirrors | Element costs use PCEC and PRICE-H. The paper reports commercial Earth delivery at $40,000/kg, SLS delivery at $46,000/kg, and the least expensive lunar case at $78,000/kg, described as 97% more expensive than Earth propellant. Those figures were not recomputed. The parametric tools are not in the paper. |

Blair's printed Version 5 statements are toolkit output under a stack of relaxed assumptions. They are not a model that can take a new scenario. They do not open the comparison gate.

The next item that would reopen the financial pair is a Sowers, Shishko, Charania, or Blair workbook, or an author-provided annual table. Another paper that repeats a headline IRR or price would not.
