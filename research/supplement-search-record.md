## Appendix A. Search record

**Date.** 29 September 2026.

**What this is.** A screened search for a second model that an independent researcher could rerun and then change. It is not a PRISMA review. The queries below are the ones actually issued. A paper that does not appear in the screening table was not adjudicated.

**Inclusion.** A candidate had to address lunar propellant, lunar or cislunar resource supply, or a space-resource business case that already answers the lunar-propellant question. It then had to pass seven items: an identifiable version, an explicit boundary, a known metric, recoverable equations or a public workbook, recoverable baseline parameters, a rerunnable headline, and inputs that can be changed. Descendants of an included lineage count as that lineage.

**Exclusion.** Mars-only ISRU, asteroid mining, and generic launch economics, unless the public model already answers the lunar-propellant question. Mass, power, or energy models with no cost, price, or value. Power-system models whose decision is a settlement's electricity. Habitat breakeven for a different customer. Results that exist only as an undigitized figure. Files the authors mark as private, confidential, or unpublished. A missing quantity was not filled in.

**Stopping rule.** Stop at three independent eligible models that can be paired, or when the log shows fewer than three. Kornuta counts as one. The second condition is the one that occurred.

**Venues.** NASA NTRS; the USRA LPI archive; publisher and DOI pages; OSF; Zenodo; GitHub; Mendeley Data; university repositories at Colorado School of Mines, UNSW, Texas Tech, and Bocconi; the SEI and NSS report archives; ICEAA slides. Scopus, Web of Science, and IEEE Xplore were not queried. Hit counts were not retained.
